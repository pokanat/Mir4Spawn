TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Know what the numbers mean.

Available now

Official rank, name, server, clan and power score. Search, filtering, CSV exports and browser favorites work with the collected ranking.

History from first capture

The collector checks every two hours. Only changed datasets create a snapshot. We retain the latest 90 snapshots. Best rank, peak power and changes refer to that retained history.

No historical backfill

We found no public player-history archive on the official ranking page. History before our first capture is unavailable.

Identity limits

Profiles are matched by exact names. A reused name may belong to someone else. Changed names are not merged. Possible transfers require a unique name in both captures.

Not in the public ranking

Level, level history, former names, equipment and detailed combat stats are unavailable here. Market listings and price history need a separate verified source.

Source and freshness

Data comes from the official MIR4 all-server ranking. Capture time and the source's ranking date are shown separately. Scheduled checks can be delayed; failed checks keep the last valid dataset.
